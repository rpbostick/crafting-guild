# Research: fast group battle, "counts as a gang", and tier or scale systems

**Resume:** an earlier run of this task was cut off before it finished. Start with `git log` and `git status` in the worktree, keep the work already there (committed or not) if it is sound, and continue from it.

Track: light

A research project for the World of Dungeons-family rules (`rules/v1/synthesis.md`; 2d6 moves, d6 damage, hit dice; Remix heroic power level by default). Write `docs/research/group-combat-and-tiers.md`.

## Questions
1. **Apocalypse World's "Not to Be Fucked With"** (the Gunlugger's move: in battle, you count as a gang, with its harm and armour, and you get +1 armour, or similar; quote the exact text and edition): how it works with AW's gang sizes (small, medium, large), harm and armour, and how "harm as established" lets one character trade with a group. Also related moves in AW, Dungeon World (the "group" and "horde" tags, the Fighter, hirelings), Apocalypse World 2e battle moves, and other PbtA games (e.g. Masks, Urban Shadows, The Sprawl, Root) where one character can stand against a group.
2. **Fast group battle systems:** rules that resolve fights between a person and a group, or group against group, in one or a few rolls: Into the Odd / Electric Bastionland detachments and "mob" rules, Cairn's detachments, Knave and Maze Rats, The Black Hack, Blades in the Dark (gang scale, effect), Forbidden Lands, Mythic Bastionland, Warlock!, OSE morale and mass combat, Swords of the Serpentine, Fate (scale), Savage Worlds (mass battles), Mausritter (warbands), Ironsworn, Freebooters on the Frontier. For each: the rule in brief, how fast it runs, what it gets right, its licence.
3. **Tier / scale / power-tag systems** that give higher-tier characters or enemies a **visible advantage** over lower tiers or over groups of lower-tier foes: e.g. Blades' scale and potency, Fate scale, Savage Worlds' Wild Cards and Extras, 13th Age mooks, D&D 4e minions, Feng Shui mooks, AW harm vs. armour by gang size, Into the Odd's detachments attacking with Enhanced damage, Cypher System levels, DW's "+n" tags. How each is shown on the sheet or stat line, how it changes rolls (effect, harm, dice size, number of attackers that count), and how it scales.

## Analysis and proposals
- What a tier tag could look like in the synthesis: a short scale (e.g. tiers 0–4 or Minion/Normal/Heroic/Legendary), what one step of difference does (damage up a die, harm reduction, counting as a gang of size N, effect level), how a group's size converts to tier, and how "Not to Be Fucked With" becomes a class ability or an optional rule in this system.
- 2–3 concrete rule drafts, each fitting on a quarter page, with a worked example of one fighter against six bandits and a party against a war band, and the expected number of rolls.
- Risks for balance with the existing hit dice and damage rules.

`docs/research/group-combat-and-tiers.md`: summary, sources cited with URLs and dates read, comparison tables, the drafts, and open questions. Quotes short, licences noted; unverified facts marked.
