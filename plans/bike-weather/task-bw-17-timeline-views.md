# Bike Weather: the outing's rain on a timeline, with the carried garments where they go on

The Today screen shows what to wear and carry through views registered in `src/screens/today/outfitViews.ts` (Shelves, the default, and List). This task adds the timeline: the outing's own 15-minute steps with the rain in each, the garments to wear from the start, and each carried garment placed on the step it would go on. Two layouts of the same data, both registered, so the existing view switch is how a person picks one. Rain only in this version: temperature and wind stay in the conditions sentence above the views. Visible: a person looks at both in a browser before the merge.

Read first: `README.md` (Today section); `src/screens/today/` (every file; `outfitViews.ts`, `ShelvesView.tsx` and its test are the pattern); `src/suggest/suggestion.ts` (`ReadySuggestion`, how `carry` gets `putOnWhen`), `src/suggest/words.ts` (`putOnWhenText`, `couldRain`, `clockTime`); `src/recommend/window.ts` (`ForecastWindow.precipSteps`, already clipped to the outing by skywright's `precipTimeline`); `node_modules/skywright/src/dryWindow.ts` (`PrecipStep`); `src/ui/` (`SegmentedChoice`, `theme.ts`, `deviceChoice.ts`); `docs/research/dark-sky.md` (the timeline section, for the look only).

## What to build
1. **The timeline view-model** (`src/suggest/timeline.ts`, with a test file): `outingTimeline(steps, startMs, endMs, carried)` returns `{ startMs, endMs, offsetMs, steps, hourMarks, carried }`. `steps`: one per precip step, with `startMs`, `endMs`, `probabilityPct`, `rateMmPerHour`, and `rain: 'none' | 'possible' | 'wet'` (`wet` above `DEFAULT_MAX_RATE_MM_PER_HOUR`, `possible` at or above `DRY_BELOW_PROBABILITY_PCT`, by the same test `putOnWhenText` makes: export `couldRain` from `words.ts` and use it; no copy). `hourMarks`: each whole hour inside the outing with its label through `clockTime`, plus the start and the end. `carried`: for each carried garment, `{ id, atStep: number | undefined, text }` where `atStep` is the index of the step the when-line names (the first step `couldRain` holds; `undefined` for "If it rains" and "Not expected to go on"), and `text` is the garment's `putOnWhen`. Add `timeline: OutingTimeline` to `ReadySuggestion`, built in `suggestToday` from `window.precipSteps` and the outing's times, so a view needs nothing but the suggestion. Tests: the step placement over the showery fixture `ShelvesView.test.tsx` uses (`RAIN_POSSIBLE`) and over a dry one; the hour marks for an outing starting on and off the hour; a garment with no step; `atStep` agrees with `putOnWhen` for every carried garment of the fixture.
2. **The strip** (`src/screens/today/TimelineStrip.tsx`, registered as `strip`, label "Strip"): time runs left to right across the width. Above the strip, the garments to wear as a wrapped row of chips (`ColorDot` and name), headed "Wear from the start". The strip: one column per step, its width in proportion to the step's length, a bar whose height is the chance (0 to 100 % of the strip's height, a hairline floor for 0) and whose fill is the theme's rain colour darkened for `wet`; the hour marks as labels under the strip at their position, start and end always shown. Below the strip, each carried garment that has a step as a pin under that step's position: a short vertical tick from the strip to a chip (`ColorDot`, name, and its `text` under it); pins that would overlap stack downward rather than overlap. Carried garments with no step come last in a wrapped row headed "Also in the pocket", each with its text. An empty pocket says `NOTHING_TO_CARRY_TEXT`.
3. **The column** (`src/screens/today/TimelineColumn.tsx`, registered as `timeline`, label "Timeline"): time runs down the screen. A narrow left column holds the rain bar, one segment per step with its height in proportion to the step's length and its width the chance; hour-mark labels sit in a gutter beside it. To the right, at the top, the garments to wear ("Wear from the start", chips); beside each pinned step, the carried garment's chip and text, aligned to the step's top; the no-step garments under "Also in the pocket" at the bottom. No scrolling inside the view: the screen already scrolls.
4. **Registry and switch.** Both views go after Shelves and List in `OUTFIT_VIEWS`; Shelves stays the default. Check that `SegmentedChoice` with four labels fits a 360 px wide phone in both themes; if it does not, make it wrap or abbreviate nothing: say in the result what you found. The saved view choice keeps working for the new ids.
5. **Accessibility and themes.** Each view has one accessible summary sentence as its label ("Rain possible from 09:00, a 40% chance; Rain jacket goes on from 09:00; nothing else in the pocket") built from the view-model in `words.ts`, tested; every chip is a labelled text element; the bar's chance is in the summary and the pin's text, never only in a colour or a height. Dark and light through `useThemeColors`; add a rain colour pair to the theme if there is none.
6. **README**: the Today section describes the four views, what the two timelines show and that they show rain only for now.

## Prior art
- Dark Sky's 24-hour timeline (`docs/research/dark-sky.md`: a bar coloured where rain falls, along a time axis) is the look both layouts follow; the column is its orientation, the strip is it turned sideways.
- Breezy Weather (LGPL-3.0, Kotlin, Android) draws an hourly precipitation trend; a design reference only, no code taken.
- `react-native-gifted-charts` (MIT) and `victory-native` (MIT; needs Skia, Reanimated and Gesture Handler) were considered for the bar and not taken: an outing is four to twelve columns, which plain `View`s draw as `ShelvesView` does, and the garment pins are not a chart feature. No package.
