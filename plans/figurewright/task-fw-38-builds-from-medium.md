## The job
The medium is the reference and does not move in this run: its overlay test must pass unchanged at the end. Every other build, every slider position and every pose is re-derived from it.

1. **Builds as transformations of the medium.** A build's widths and profiles are the medium's, changed by the relations the ANSUR fits give between builds (how chest breadth, upper-arm girth and stature move the acromion, the deltoid, the arm's top and the torso stations), applied as ratios or offsets around the medium's values, not as absolute fitted numbers. Write the rule once, where the body is built from sliders, with its source in the comment. At neutral sliders the result is the medium exactly (a test).
2. **Ranges and scale.** Widen `MEASUREMENT_RANGES` so the medium sits inside them with room on both sides (it is at or near the minimum for ankle, neck and shoulder width now), with each new end stated relative to the medium. Re-base the head scale on the new medium so other builds' heads no longer clamp at 1.15.
3. **Shoulders on every build.** The cap is the traced arc on every build and slider corner. Settle what the earlier runs left open, by rule rather than per build: thin arms (the slim corners where the outline turned in under the cap by up to 30°; the acromion cover may shrink or go, since the traced cap no longer needs it to start inside the arm) and the step where the broad near arm's flat top meets the cap. Tests over all 512 slider corners: no turn-in under the cap beyond a stated angle, no step at the arm's top, the armpit gap rule, the no-spike torso, the arm's top at or above the armpit.
4. **Other poses.** Every pose rig still poses every build with the same rules holding; where the easing rig's joints moved, say whether the other rigs needed the same correction and why or why not. `splash-easing`'s rig overlay draws the joint positions as drawn on the source art.
5. **Garments.** Raise `ARM_TOP_REACH` to what the new medium needs (at least 0.55, with the measured value in its comment), take the import reference from the new medium, re-import the tee and zip jacket and `assets/garments/*.json`, recheck the deviation tests (the 0.5 cm bound stays) and the layering thickness tests on every build.
6. **Pinned numbers** in the 84 failing tests: recheck each against the new geometry and re-pin with the measured value; where a bound was a rule (a range from ANSUR, the 12° rule, hands within 2 cm), decide from the rule whether it still applies around an art-defined medium, and say so in the test. List in your result every rule you dropped or changed, with old and new numbers.
7. **Renders.** Re-pin the byte pins after looking at the renders, `npm run renders:approve`, and regenerate the before/after page (before = main, after = this branch): stage A's overlay block stays at the top; then the build lineup bare and dressed, every changed sheet, and 3× close-ups of the near shoulder and armpit and the far arm on broad, round, short and round, tallest, slim and the slimmest 140 cm corner.

## Commands
One command per Bash call, each check through the evidence runner: `npx jest --runInBand <files>`, `npx tsc --noEmit`, `npx tsx <file>.ts [args]`, `npm run renders:sheets`, `npm run renders:approve`, `node`, `cp`. No `env VAR=…` prefix, `git archive`, `tar`, `git stash`, `git checkout` or `git restore`: they are denied; pass options as arguments, and undo a change with Edit. Do not run `npm install`. Hold one raster at a time; run long render jobs in the foreground, split if needed.

## Done when
Every build and slider corner is derived from the medium by the stated rule; the medium's overlay test passes unchanged; `npx jest --runInBand` over the whole `test/` directory and tsc pass; the assets are re-imported and the goldens re-approved; the before/after page is regenerated; and the result lists every rule or bound that changed.
