## figurewright: the pelvis-to-thigh join from the library bodies

The 2D body's hips and thighs meet at a join that the viewer reads as wrong on many builds: the pelvis looks deformed, like an anatomical study gone off, and the thighs start too narrow for the hips. The library bodies (`assets/bodies/`, nine human bodies measured off a 3D model, read by `src/body/library.ts` and `scripts/body-library.ts`) carry a measured pelvis-to-thigh outline for each build in each pose. This step makes the 2D body's join follow that outline, on the library bodies and the named builds alike, with pants following. It is visible: a person looks at the page before the merge.
