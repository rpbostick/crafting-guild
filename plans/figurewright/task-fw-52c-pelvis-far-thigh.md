# figurewright: resume the pelvis join: the far thigh on its bone, then the rest

Third run of this step in the same worktree. The branch (tip 6d81ed4) holds the library join (`libraryJoin`, `LIBRARY_JOIN`), the `pelvisJoin` measure, the pants following, and a README that records why re-fitting the medium under the join did not work: in the easing pose the join draws the far thigh centred 1.6 cm outward of its bone halfway down (8.81 cm out, 5.69 in, where easing-2's is 7.06 and 7.81, within 0.4 cm of the bone), and no body width or joint the fit can move corrects that. The re-fit's values were not written back, rightly: they break the forearms, the near lower leg and the ankle.

**The rule this run follows.** A build's width at the hips and thighs is flesh around the bone, not a bone moved outward. On a straight build the abdomen runs almost straight into the pelvis; the rounder builds make the curve by adding fat and muscle on the leg, the hip and the torso, and move the leg bones little if at all (a pelvis-width slider could move them, later, not now). So each thigh's outline is centred on its femur in every pose, near and far, and the join's width is carried as flesh either side of the bone by the library's proportions. The overlay bounds in `test/body/easing-overlay.test.ts` are not loosened.
