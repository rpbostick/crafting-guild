# figurewright: the 3D easing pose without its chest roll, and the 2D rig re-fitted to it

The body library's easing pose (`tools/body3d/poses/easing.json`, "fitted to easing-2 on the average build") rolls the chest about 17° in the front view: the far shoulder sits 7 to 9 cm below the near one on all nine bodies (`test/body-library/far-shoulder.test.ts` holds the numbers), while base and hands on hips are level. The easing-2 drawing's shoulders tilt only about 3°. The 2D easing rig was fitted with a 15° shoulder tilt that matches the roll, not the drawing. Decided: **the reference loses the roll, and the rig follows the drawing.** The torso's turn and lean stay; only the roll goes. Visible: a person looks at a guided page before the merge.

Read first: `tools/body3d/README.md`, `generate.py`, `arm_rule.py`, `poses/easing.json` and `base.json` (axis-angle rotations in radians per bone: `root`, `spine01` to `spine03`, the clavicles); `assets/bodies/` and `src/body/library.ts`; `test/body-library/far-shoulder.test.ts`; `scripts/easing-search.ts`, `scripts/easing-overlay.ts`, `assets/poses/easing.json` (the 2D rig), `src/body/pose/posed.ts` (`shoulderJoint`, `hangFromArmpit`, how easing places its shoulder joint from the armpit); `test/body/easing-overlay.test.ts`, `easing-shoulders.test.ts`, `shoulder-width.test.ts`; `spike/easing-shoulders/transforms.json`.
