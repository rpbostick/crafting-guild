## Requirements (Patrick, 2026-09-27; see `plans/bike-weather/product.md`)
- **Shoulders follow the chest** (Patrick, 2026-10-01): the shoulder anchors to the chest, so shoulder width comes mainly from chest breadth; thicker arms add a little. Garments follow the body's shoulder, so fix shoulders in the body, not in the garments.
- **Arms hang from the armpit** (Patrick, 2026-10-01): the shoulder's anchor is the armpit; a hanging arm meets the torso at the armpit with no gap. Shoulder options round 1 → C (slope + width from chest and arm), "and go farther": round 2 `fw-shoulder-armpit`. Round 2 (2026-10-01): Patrick picked **E**, rounder toward the source art; armpit stays at 0.735; shoulders slider → ±1.5 cm deltoid offset; the round builds' midsection spike is back (belly beside the far forearm) and must go. Build: `fw-shoulders-build` (gate).
- An open-source (MIT) avatar tool in TypeScript for React Native/Expo, with a small Expo demo app, released separately from Bike Weather, which consumes it.
- Users make an avatar that looks like them: **body shapes** (builds: slim to broad, height, chest/waist/hips) in one standing pose now, more poses (e.g. riding) later.
- colorwright's head parts (head styles, hair, faces, facial hair, hats, accessories; Open Peeps CC0 art) on top, with colorwright's colour roles, skin, hat/hair covering and draw order, ported to TypeScript. The art and colorwright's tests are in `vendor/colorwright/` (commit b527691).
- A **garment layering engine** and a basic set of **parametric garments** (tee, long sleeve, jacket, pants, shorts, shoes; generated in code to fit every body shape, recolourable). Bike Weather adds its own closed catalog (cycling gear, ratings) on top of the engine.
- An illustrator may redraw garments later if the app sells; the engine must accept hand-drawn garment art as well as generated.

- **Real layering** (Patrick, 2026-09-28): clothing layers like real clothing: undergarments, base, mid, insulation, shell, coats; hats, snowpants, gloves, etc. Several colour-coded garments worn at once, lower layers visible under upper ones (collars, cuffs, hems; an open/cutaway view), layers removable and addable in a tidy, correct order. Future animation: "get dressed", "get undressed", "remove/add a layer", garments exploding out and flying back like FreeCell's win screen. Design implications: zones with ordered layer ranks and several garments per zone; each layer fits over what is beneath it; a dressing-order rule (what must come off first); `renderFigure` output as body + one group per garment with stable ids and anchors, plus an exploded-layout function (target transforms per garment in layer order); the app animates, the engine supplies positions and order; hand-drawn garments use the same model.
Guesses (tell Patrick if one turns out wrong): renders are SVG strings (react-native-svg in the app, resvg for tests and contact sheets); the body is drawn in the Open Peeps line style (black outline, flat fills) so heads and body match.

## Decisions
- 2026-09-29, on the import spike (Patrick): imported garments sit a fixed **cm past the skin** on every body; build a **dedicated shoulder-arc frame** in the first step; details under about **¼ of the segment width are rigid**; Open Peeps: "what is going to help us the most in the future", since more art will be imported, so the found-art path gets automated (I5) rather than done by hand; parametric garments stay as the ground-truth check.
- 2026-09-27: MIT; name figurewright; a new project that ports colorwright's parts (colorwright unchanged); body shapes now, poses later; parametric garments, illustrator later; engine + basic garments open, catalog and ratings closed in Bike Weather (Patrick).
- 2026-09-27, on the spike (Patrick): **variable-width ink** lines; **reuse Open Peeps' hands, feet and shoes** (CC0, cut from the vendored poses); **heads grow mildly with shoulder width**; **one straight-on pose now**, variants (weight shift, hand in pocket) later alongside the riding pose.
- Orchestrator defaults, not asked: users enter height plus a few sliders (build, shoulders, chest, waist, hips, limb thickness); tape circumferences later if wanted. A three-quarter head on the straight-on body is accepted, with colorwright's mirroring.
- 2026-09-28, on the easing and body-analysis spikes (Patrick): arms stop at **12° and lie in front** on wide bodies; arm clearance **2 cm** from the frame; keep easing's **4–5% longer near limbs**; keep the near **sleeve line but broken with a gap** like easing's jacket (not a solid seam); **belly keeps the full range** (0.32 of height with build +1); **keep the belly crease**; build +1 round in **belly, hips and thighs** with ordinary shoulders and chest; average waist **29 cm**; wrists and ankles thinned 12–18%, **checked against human data**; **shoes scale with height**. Before the build, a **reference spike**: pointing_finger and resting as the natural-body guides plus real human references (ANSUR II measurements and openly licensed photos, photos never committed), mapped to our skeleton, "Open Peeps style with human references"; knee height comes from it. **No riding pose** for now: cycling clothes on the regular pose (the app may become a multi-activity "Hike Weather" or similar; name after SEO).
- Orchestrator defaults, not asked: commit the seven-bodies check render as `docs/renders/`-style evidence in the build step; bake the hands' evenodd clip into the skin path in the build step.
- 2026-09-28, on body-refs (Patrick): round bodies keep pointing_finger's **belly overhang**; broad-shoulder extreme stays **47 cm** (stylised, past ANSUR's 43); round and pear builds' **thighs touch** near the crotch; **search again** for photos from other openly licensed sources.
- 2026-09-28 (Patrick): **Open Peeps style over realism**: figures cover a wide range of bodies with a nice stylistic flourish; human references set ranges, not the look. BodyM skipped for good. Height 140–200; round hips and belly widened together.
- 2026-09-28 (Patrick, on the rebuilt builds): torso sides smooth (no spikes on round bodies); armpit height from the torso, not the arm width; shoulders rounded and down-sloping like Open Peeps; widths scale with height so an average body stays average at any height; the knee at 0.6 × inseam accepted. **Later body controls** (not yet): neck length and width; separate arm and leg thickness; pelvis/hip depth, abdomen, chest, bust, shoulders. "We may be able to get away with simplicity" for now.
- 2026-09-28, on the layering spike (Patrick): **priority is functional, good-looking dressed avatars**; animation and options are fun on top: build the engine to allow them, no feature creep. Sizing accumulates over layers through one `thickness` per garment (no per-garment variants). Hundreds or thousands of garments must come through an **import engine** (one piece of art + a few anchors per garment), so the garment import step comes right after compose. Cutaway: both views, the app picks (opened fronts when every torso layer zips, steps otherwise). Exploding: default piles, tops to a small pile top right and bottoms to one bottom right; swipe tops to the top pile, bottoms to the bottom pile, around the belt for both; user-chosen zones later (app gestures; the engine supplies pile targets and order). Per-garment defaults the user can flip (tuck, gloves under/over cuffs); buff under collars, scarf over the coat; flying garments relax to their free shape.

- 2026-09-29 (Patrick, on the fw-dress2 and splash previews): the pink and the Mohawk head style are fine; **keep the hems as they are**, including the jersey showing in a small pocket below the jacket hem on round and short-and-round bodies (the jersey is longer than the jacket). The tee's far-hip V (far forearm under the tee) is fine.
