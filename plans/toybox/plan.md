## Later builds
- **Eyedropper, rebuilt (Patrick, 2026-10-08; after the 10/12 budget reset).** The first one was removed in 0.7.0: in Firefox it sampled only element backgrounds on a click and showed nothing while hovering. Wanted: a crosshair cursor pointing at the colour on screen, a small preview circle that follows the pointer and shows the hovered colour in real time, and a click puts the colour into the Colour picker (and recent colours). **Ours in every browser**, Chromium included, instead of the native EyeDropper (same look in demos). How to read the screen is still open: a snapshot of the visible page when picking starts (modern-screenshot / html-to-image / html2canvas-pro, all MIT, lazily loaded; the backgrounds' WebGL canvases grabbed in the same frame) versus screen capture via getDisplayMedia (exact, but a permission prompt every time). Research spike first; normal track, gate for Patrick to try it on a preview (~$10-15).
